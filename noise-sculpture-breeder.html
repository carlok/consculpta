    Each sculpture is the region where a formula over 3D simplex noise rises above an iso level inside a sphere, cube or drum. Formulas are trees of noise, trigonometry and space transforms (twist, mirror, rotate, warp). Every offspring changes its parent's tree by tweaking a number, swapping a function, grafting a new branch, wrapping a node or cutting one out. About one offspring in twelve switches to strands: thin filaments traced where the formula's surface crosses a rotated copy of itself. The page evaluates each formula on 520 sample points to normalise it, estimate a safe raymarching step and throw out empty or solid results before anything is compiled.
    
    
    Simplex noise by Ian McEwan, Ashima Arts and Stefan Gustavson (MIT). Colours use Inigo Quilez's cosine palettes. Shader code exports a standalone Shadertoy shader with the formula compiled in.
